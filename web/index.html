      How scoring works

      1. Tap a player's card at the top — the colored outline shows who's pegging.

      2. Tap what they scored (15, Pair, Go…) — their peg advances instantly.

      3. Use 🃏 Count hand to tally a full hand from the cards, and ↩︎ Undo to fix any mis-peg.
